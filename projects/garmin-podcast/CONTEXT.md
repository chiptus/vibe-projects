# Garmin Podcast

Podcasts on a Garmin watch, built by hand-picking Episodes into a Playlist that the watch Syncs over Wi-Fi.

## Language

**Podcast**: A show, identified by its RSS feed.
**Episode**: One audio item of a Podcast.
**Library**: The Podcasts a person has saved. Never shared between people.
**Playlist**: The ordered list of Episodes a person picked for the watch. Hand-built. Avoid "Flow".
**Sync**: The watch fetching its Playlist and audio, and reporting back.
**Storage budget**: How much watch space a Sync may use; the oldest Episodes by publish date are left out beyond it.
**Pairing code**: Short-lived, single-use code the watch shows so a signed-in person can link it.
**Search provider**: A source of Podcast search results (iTunes first).
**Listening position**: Where the person stopped in an Episode (later).
